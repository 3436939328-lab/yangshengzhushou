# Seasonal Background Assets

Four photos are packaged under `entry/src/main/resources/base/media`, so normal display does not contact an image host.

Source: Unsplash image CDN, downloaded 2026-09-08 under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license).

Season boundaries follow the calculated instants of Beginning of Spring, Summer, Autumn and Winter in China Standard Time. The photo covers the entire window, including the system safe areas behind transparent status/navigation bars. Header, tab bar and loading-page backgrounds are transparent; white labels use shadows for contrast. Content cards and input surfaces retain their readable backgrounds. Only the image expands into safe areas, keeping controls clear of system bars and cutouts.

Solar-term timestamps are generated by lunar-javascript 1.7.7 (MIT), https://github.com/6tail/lunar-javascript. Its license is included in the application's raw resources. The generation script and pinned tool dependency manifest are retained in the workspace; runtime uses static timestamps without requiring Node or network access.
